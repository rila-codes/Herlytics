🌸 HERLYTICS SUMMARY
🌸 HerLytics 

 

Predict. Prevent. Empower. 

 

Executive Summary 

 

HerLytics is an AI-powered, open-source women's wellness platform designed to help women understand their health through predictive analytics, personalized wellness guidance, and lifestyle support. 

Unlike traditional period-tracking applications, HerLytics focuses on early risk prediction rather than diagnosis. Its flagship feature is an AI-powered PCOS (PCOD) Risk Prediction system that estimates the likelihood of PCOS based on user-provided lifestyle, menstrual, and health information. 

Important: HerLytics is not a medical diagnostic tool. It is a wellness platform that promotes awareness and encourages timely consultation with healthcare professionals. 

 

🎯 Vision 

 

To become the world's most trusted AI-powered women's wellness platform by combining artificial intelligence, healthcare knowledge, and intuitive user experiences to empower women at every stage of life. 

 

🎯 Mission 

 

Empower women with intelligent health insights that encourage preventive healthcare, healthier lifestyle choices, and informed decision-making.

Millions of women experience symptoms related to PCOS but often recognize them only after they become more severe.

Calorie counting

Actionable next steps

⚠ Medical Disclaimer 

HerLytics is not a diagnostic or treatment platform. Users with concerning symptoms or elevated risk should consult a qualified healthcare professional. 

 

 

🌟 Unique Selling Proposition (USP) 

 

HerLytics goes beyond tracking health data. It helps users understand, act, and improve through a seamless journey: 

Predict → AI estimates the likelihood of PCOS risk. 

Prevent → Personalized nutrition, healthy habits, and educational guidance support preventive care. 

Empower → Clear insights and practical tools enable women to make informed decisions about their health. 

 

Instead of stopping at information, HerLytics focuses on turning insights into everyday actions, making it more than a tracker—it becomes a comprehensive, AI-powered wellness companion designed to support women throughout different stages of their health journey.
